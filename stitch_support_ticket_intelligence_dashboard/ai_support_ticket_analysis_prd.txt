# Product Requirements Document
## AI Support Ticket Analysis System

**Status:** MVP implemented
**Version:** 1.0
**Owner:** Support Operations / Engineering
**Last Updated:** 2026-09-16

### Key Capabilities & Scope
- **Dataset:** 500 tickets loaded from `support_tickets.csv` (FastAPI backend + Pandas).
- **KPI Metrics:** Total, Open, Critical, Resolved, Escalated ticket counts, SLA breach rates, and CSAT.
- **Natural-Language Querying (Groq LLM + Deterministic Rule Fallback):** Plain-English queries, preset questions, transparent `[llm | rule-based]` attribution tag.
- **Anomaly Detection:** Outlier resolution times, unresolved high-priority tickets, escalated unresolved bottlenecks, high response times with configurable day filter (`days=30`).
- **Agent Resolution & Leaderboard:** Agent performance metrics (resolved count, average CSAT rating, resolution times).
- **REST Endpoints:** `/`, `/api/health`, `/api/query`, `/api/anomalies`, `/api/stats`, `/api/agents`.
